The morning after completing our Camino, we woke at 11am, after significantly more sleep than I'd had in over a month. This threw us off of our original plan, but we nevertheless got ready to go to pilgrim's mass at the cathedral, an essential post-Camino experience. The queue to get in was very long, and we had to stand near the back. The mass itself was also very long, and we did not understand much of what was happening as it was all in Spanish. Lawrence started fidgeting a lot, and we left.

We continued being unproductive, and went back to the hotel room where Lawrence and Woody started watching a stupid YouTube video. I left to ask at the hotel reception if they would be able to receive a parcel for me. Before coming on the Camino, I had not planned any post-Camino activities, but we had arrived in Santiago de Compostela with a full week before I would fly back to the UK, so I planned to go on a road trip around Galicia. Unfortunately I had not brought my driver's license or my credit card with me, so I needed my parents to send them to my hotel.

After speaking to the hotel, I went to a park nearby and called Alex, who I missed even more now that I was no longer walking. Soon the guys started nagging me to join them, and we went to a laundromat and then an ice cream shop while we waited for our washing to be done.

To finish off the day, we all went to Nils and Laura's Airbnb for drinks, snacks and games. The guys shaved the facial hair they'd grown on the Camino, but Nils left a stupid moustache and pretended to be Luigi. We also tried to do a 3-person piggyback, which was somewhat a success. Woody played us Vroom Vroom by Charli XCX and we all loved it so much we played it on repeat for the rest of the evening.

As it got late, I was encouraging the guys to drink more wine, but Lawrence started to seriously complain that he was feeling very full and unwell. We left to go back to our hotel, but when we arrived, Lawrence ran straight into the bathroom to throw up. Unfortunately this was the first of hundreds of very loud episodes that occurred in the ensuite every 10 minutes throughout the entire night, while Woody and I lay staring at the ceiling in our beds, unable to do anything to help. Truly this was the most harrowing of possible ends to the Camino.

The next day, we finally all went our separate ways. Woody left early to go to Madrid, leaving me to help Lawrence transfer to his new hotel room. I brought him more water so he wouldn't have to leave the bed for any reason, and said goodbye. Nils and Laura also planned to do some sightseeing in Galicia, so although I said goodbye to them, I knew I would see them in only a couple of days. The farewells were anticlimatic, but I knew I'd be seeing my new lifelong friends again soon.

I went to the airport, collected my rental car, and travelled on to my next adventure.
