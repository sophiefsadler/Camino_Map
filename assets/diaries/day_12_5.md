When I arrived in Burgos with my friends, I said goodbye and walked to the train station where I would take a train to Madrid to meet my boyfriend, Alex, for two days. You can see a gap between the two line segments since the train station was significantly off-course from the Camino, so I only tracked the parts I walked officially on the route!

While in Madrid, we went sightseeing, ate churros and relaxed in the parks. Meanwhile, Lawrence, Woody and Nils spent a day resting in Burgos, and on the second day, walked a short 11km to the town of Tardajos. On my return, I would have to walk the same section to Tardajos and then continue to Hontanas, where we would all be meeting the next day.

When I returned to Burgos after my days in Madrid, I took the best route I could come up with which moved me in the right direction while bringing me back to the Camino. You can see on the map the spot where I rejoined the correct route!
